# Junjie Nian (年骏杰)

I am an undergraduate AI researcher at **FudanNLP Alex Lab, Fudan University**, advised by [Prof. Yixin Cao](https://sites.google.com/view/yixin-homepage), and a 2026–2027 exchange student in Computer Science & Engineering at **UC San Diego**.

I work on **reasoning models, AI agents, and interpretability**—understanding how models reason, where agent trajectories fail, and how internal signals can guide better decisions. My recent publications include [ARM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07309) at **EMNLP 2026** and [Thinking Traps in Long Chain-of-Thought](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1930.pdf) in **Findings of ACL 2026**.
